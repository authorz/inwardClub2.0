# Product

## Register

product

## Users

InwardClub 2.0 serves members of an offline club/store operation, store staff, store operators, and headquarters operators. The mini program is used by members on mobile while choosing stores, ordering, booking tables/seats, buying events, managing tickets/coupons, viewing wallet activity, and accessing member benefits. Staff use limited mini-program workflows for verification and point review.

## Product Purpose

The product connects offline store membership operations with mobile self-service: store discovery, ordering, reservations, event purchase, ticket/coupon usage, member wallet, invitation, rankings, and staff verification. Success means members can complete high-frequency actions quickly, while all payments, wallet changes, reservations, and verifications remain traceable and governed by server-side rules.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, premium, confident. The interface should feel like a modern private club tool: calm, precise, spacious, and operationally trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Do not look like a generic colorful restaurant mini program, coupon marketplace, noisy e-commerce app, or card-heavy SaaS dashboard. Avoid decorative card grids, loud gradients, childish icon sets, cramped promo blocks, excessive badges, and visual clutter.

## Design Principles

1. Let workflows lead: each screen should make the next action obvious without explaining the app.
2. Use restraint as the brand: black, white, grayscale, generous spacing, strong typography, and quiet hierarchy.
3. Avoid card dependency: use bands, lists, dividers, full-width sections, tabs, bottom sheets, and editorial composition instead of stacked cards.
4. Preserve trust around money and assets: orders, coupons, tickets, wallet, and verification states must be explicit and hard to misread.
5. Design for repeated use: common actions should be thumb-friendly, fast to scan, and tolerant of edge states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast. Body text must remain readable on grayscale and gradient surfaces. Motion should be minimal and respect reduced-motion settings. Do not rely on color alone for paid, used, expired, pending, or failed states.
